### Motivation and Background
The telecommunications market is often competitive as customers can easily switch providers if unsatisfied with their service
The reason customers switch providers is varied and many
Telecommunications companies have to fight for their piece of marketshare

### Stakeholders for this investigation
- The decision-makers of this particular telecommunications company, high power high interest stakeholders in adjusting company services to decrease customer churn.
- The customers of this telecommunications company, low power high interest stakeholders, they cannot directly change the service but are interested in improving it to better suits their needs.

### Problem Definition
Customers of Telecom services are switching providers/churning causing loss of recurring revenue

### Objective
Develop an end-to-end data engineering and machine learning pipeline to predict customer churn and investigate the factors associated with customer churn.

### Practical Relevance
By investigating the reasons linked with churn this particular telecommunications company can focus their efforts on improving on these
particular features to reduce customer churn, which will potentially decrease the loss of recurring revenue
